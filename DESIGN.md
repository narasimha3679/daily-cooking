# Design System: Daily Cooking

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Kitchen Ledger"**

Daily Cooking is a working kitchen book, not a recipe catalogue. The screen is cream paper, the masthead is deep green ink, and the names of the dishes are set in a local serif as if they were written into a family notebook. Terracotta marks the thing to do next: add, swap, buy, choose. The week is ruled like a ledger, with large tabular dates in the margin and dish rows that read from top to bottom. The shopping list carries the same logic, with the total in the main column and the practical buy amount in terracotta beside it.

The system is built for one thumb in a kitchen or a shop. Controls are 44 px or larger, the primary destinations live in a fixed bottom bar, and the search and filters stay pinned while the list scrolls. It is warm without becoming decorative: there is no photography, no glass, no gradient, no external font or icon pack. The warmth comes from paper, ink, spice colour and type. The phone is the primary surface. Desktop is the same system given a left rail and wider editorial cards.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Deep green structure, warm cream paper, terracotta action, turmeric only for "Today" and notes.
- Georgia for food names, dates, numerals and section headings. A system humanist sans for controls and meta.
- Ruled groups, tabular numerals and a visible total / buy pair in every shopping row.
- A fixed 66 px bottom bar on the phone, a 232 to 250 px left rail on desktop.
- Soft warm shadows, full 1 px borders, no one-sided accent bars.
- Every state is visible: Loved, Not for me, Planned, Today, picked, full, disabled, empty, loading, error.

## Colors

The palette is a spice-box palette on cream paper. Green carries the structure and the food names, terracotta carries action and the current moment, turmeric is a small seasonal accent, and the creams carry everything else.

### Primary
- **Deep green** (#123528): the masthead, the main ink for dish names and group headings, the active rail item and the checked checkbox.
- **Deep green soft** (#174233): the dark active fill on the desktop navigation and the checked state.
- **Deep green rule** (#1f5340): borders on dark surfaces and the progress fill.

### Secondary
- **Terracotta** (#b4492e): the primary button, the add button, the active tab marker and the category label.
- **Deep terracotta** (#963a24): the buy amount, destructive text, the focus ring and the date numeral.
- **Terracotta wash** (#f6dfd4): the Loved badge and the selected Not for me button.

### Tertiary
- **Turmeric** (#c98a1e): the small rule on the icon and the steam in the app mark. Used sparingly.
- **Turmeric wash** (#fbf0d8): the Today pill and the source-note panel.

### Neutral
- **Cream ground** (#fbf5e8): the page. Pinned by the product brief. Not a default beige.
- **Cream raised** (#fffcf5): the navigation bar, chips and secondary buttons.
- **Cream card** (#fffdf8): recipe cards, day cards, sheets and shopping rows.
- **Cream deep** (#f4ebd9): muted backgrounds and the app icon ground.
- **Ink** (#22201b): body copy and shopping item names.
- **Ink soft** (#5a5346): meta, hints, provenance, checked item names and months.
- **Hairline** (#ddd0b9): decorative separators.
- **Hairline strong** (#8a7d68): control borders that need a 3:1 boundary.
- **Loved surface** (#fff8e6) and **loved line** (#e3c783): the warm highlight on a Loved card.

### Named Rules
**The Paper Rule.** Cards are warm paper on cream. Never pure white on grey, and never a grey card inside another card.

**The Two-Ink Rule.** Deep green owns structure and names. Terracotta owns action and the present moment. Do not swap them.

**The Cream Exception.** The cream ground is a deliberate, brief-pinned material, not a reflex. Keep it warm and pair it with green and terracotta, never with blue or purple.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Georgia, with Iowan Old Style, Palatino Linotype, Book Antiqua and Times New Roman as local fallbacks.
**Body Font:** Segoe UI, with system-ui, -apple-system, Helvetica Neue and Arial as local fallbacks.
**Label Font:** The same humanist sans, set small and tracked.

**Character:** A quiet cookbook pairing. The serif gives the food names, dates and steps a printed warmth. The sans keeps controls, quantities and metadata plain and legible at arm's length. There is no external font request and no Inter.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400 to 700, 24 to 28 px, 1.05 to 1.15): the masthead wordmark, the page title and the recipe sheet title.
- **Title** (700, 19.5 to 23 px, 1.15 to 1.2): dish names, day headings and shopping group headings.
- **Body** (400, 14.5 to 17 px, 1.5): recipe steps, notes and empty-state copy.
- **Meta** (400, 12 to 13.5 px, 1.4): time, base servings, ingredient count, provenance and hints.
- **Label** (650 to 700, 11 to 11.5 px, 0.1em, uppercase): category, Loved, Not for me, Today and the small overlines inside sheets.
- **Numeral** (700, 14 to 28 px, tabular): dates, slot numbers, total quantities and buy amounts.
- **Micro** (700, 9.5 px, 0.1em): the decorative month abbreviation in the planner date block only. It is duplicated by the full day heading and never carries unique information.

### Named Rules
**The One Display Voice Rule.** The serif is for names, dates, section headings and step numerals. It never sets a button, a filter or a form label.

**The 11 px Floor Rule.** No functional text below 11 px. The month abbreviation in the planner date block is decorative and duplicated by the day heading.

**The Tabular Number Rule.** Quantities, dates and buy amounts always use tabular numerals. A shopping column must line up.

## Layout

The phone is the design target. At 360 to 430 px the page uses 16 px gutters, 12 px card gaps, a 64 to 67 px masthead and a fixed 66 px bottom bar. The view reserves the bar height at the bottom. The search and filter toolbar is sticky at the top, with an opaque cream background and a hairline. Controls are 44 px minimum; the recipe cards, day rows and shopping rows are 60 to 64 px tall.

At 560 px the gutters grow to 22 px. At 640 px two-column grids begin for recipes and day cards. At 900 px the shell becomes a 232 px left rail plus content, and the bottom bar becomes a vertical rail. The recipes run in two wide editorial columns. At 1120 px the rail grows to 250 px. The grid never overflows horizontally. The 200 percent text zoom keeps the page at 390 px with no clipping.

The spacing rhythm is 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32 px. More space sits above a heading than below it. Sticky regions are separated from content by a full 1 px rule, never a floating shadow alone.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is paper, not glass. Surfaces are flat at rest with a full 1 px warm hairline and a soft shadow that has both an offset and a blur. Shadows are warm green-black, never a zero-offset coloured halo. The recipe sheet and the picker rise from the bottom on a phone and centre on desktop. The sticky toolbar is opaque, so content never shows through it.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Paper rest** (`0 14px 30px -24px rgba(18, 53, 40, 0.55), 0 2px 5px rgba(18, 53, 40, 0.05)`): recipe cards, day cards and the week actions panel.
- **Paper lift** (`0 18px 36px -22px rgba(18, 53, 40, 0.5), 0 4px 9px rgba(18, 53, 40, 0.07)`): hovered cards.
- **Sheet** (`0 24px 70px -30px rgba(13, 40, 29, 0.75)`): the detail and picker sheets.
- **Bottom bar** (`0 -14px 32px -26px rgba(18, 53, 40, 0.6)`): the fixed phone navigation.

## Shapes

Corners are gently cut, not pill-shaped everywhere. Cards and sheets use 14 to 18 px. Inputs, icon buttons and secondary controls use 10 px. Chips, badges and the Today marker are full pills. Borders are full 1 px lines in a warm hairline or a stronger control line. Group headings carry a full 2 px deep green rule. The masthead carries a 1 px terracotta bottom rule. There are no one-sided accent bars, no nested cards and no rounded icon tiles above headings. Icons are drawn on a 24 px grid with a 1.8 px round stroke.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 10 to 12 px radius, 48 px minimum height, 18 px horizontal padding.
- **Primary:** terracotta ground, cream text, soft shadow. The add action and the sheet primary.
- **Secondary:** paper ground, deep green text, 1.5 px strong hairline. Print, copy, week actions.
- **Danger ghost:** transparent ground, deep terracotta text and a terracotta wash on hover.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover deepens the ground or border. Focus is a 3 px deep terracotta ring with a 2 px offset. On dark green surfaces the ring switches to cream.

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, 44 px minimum, paper ground, 1.5 px strong hairline, 14 px semibold deep green text.
- **State:** selected is a deep green fill with cream text. Pressed state is exposed with aria-pressed.

### Cards
- **Recipe card:** paper ground, 14 px radius, full hairline, paper rest shadow. Category label, serif name, time, base servings and ingredient count. A planned pill when the dish is in the week. Loved is a warm surface and a filled heart. Not for me is a muted surface, a badge and a Restore action.
- **Day card:** paper ground, 14 px radius, full hairline. A 28 px terracotta date numeral and month in the margin, a serif day heading, numbered dish rows, 44 px swap and remove controls, and a full-width add row. Today gets a 2 px terracotta ring and a turmeric pill.
- **Shopping row:** 64 px minimum, full-width clickable label, 44 px checkbox hit area, 28 px drawn box, item name and provenance, right-aligned total and a 12 px terracotta buy amount. Checked is a green wash, a filled check and a strike-through.
- **Corner Style:** 14 px on cards, 18 px on sheets.
- **Internal Padding:** 13 to 16 px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Search:** paper ground, 1.5 px strong hairline, 48 px height, 12 px radius, a drawn search icon at 20 px and a 16 px input to prevent iOS zoom.
- **Focus:** deep terracotta border plus a 3 px deep terracotta ring at a 2 px offset.
- **Select:** 44 px, 16 px text, a drawn chevron, no native arrow.

### Navigation
- **Phone:** a fixed 66 px cream bar with three items, drawn icons and 11.5 px labels. The active item is deep green with a 46 px terracotta marker at the top. The bar respects the bottom safe area.
- **Desktop:** a 232 to 250 px rail. The masthead is a deep green card. The three destinations are vertical 54 px rows; the active row is a deep green fill with cream text.
- **Header:** a deep green masthead with a drawn pot mark, a 24 px serif wordmark, an 11 px tracked sublabel and a 44 px outlined Week chip.

### Sheets
- **Phone:** a bottom sheet, 94dvh maximum, 20 px top corners, a paper ground, a fixed head and action bar, a scrollable body. The body is focusable so PageDown and arrow keys scroll it.
- **Desktop:** a centred 720 px sheet with 18 px corners and a 90dvh maximum.
- **Motion:** one authored moment, a 280 ms rise from 18 px and 99 percent scale with an exponential ease-out. Reduced motion removes it.
- **Actions:** one full-width primary, then Loved and Not for me. The close control is 44 px and stays at 44 px with long titles.

### Signature: The Ledger Week
The planner's date block is the signature. A 28 px terracotta numeral and a tracked month sit in a narrow margin, separated from the dish rows by a full hairline. The day heading names the full date. The shopping list repeats the same discipline in its group rules and its total / buy column.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use deep green for structure and names, cream paper for surfaces and terracotta for actions and the current moment.
- **Do** set food names, dates, section headings and step numerals in the local serif stack.
- **Do** keep every control at 44 px minimum, every body text at 4.5:1 or better, and every functional label at 11 px or larger.
- **Do** keep search and filters sticky, keep the three destinations in a fixed bottom bar on the phone, and keep the servings control beside the plan or the list it changes.
- **Do** show a total and a practical buy amount in every shopping row, and show the checked state with a wash, a check and a strike-through.
- **Do** scale ingredient amounts to the chosen servings in the recipe sheet, while keeping the source amounts behind a disclosure.
- **Do** keep Not for me dishes visible in Recipes with a Restore action, and hide them from planner picks.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use purple to blue gradients, Inter, pure white cards on grey, grey cards inside cards, glass, gradient text or rounded icon tiles above headings.
- **Don't** use hot pink, amber or any off-palette accent. Turmeric is the only warm third accent.
- **Don't** use one-sided accent bars, hard offset shadows, or a zero-offset coloured halo.
- **Don't** add external fonts, photography, CDN links or runtime network calls. The app works offline.
- **Don't** auto-generate a plan, change the shopping aggregation, or hide a Not for me dish from the Recipes list.
- **Don't** use em dashes in site copy or code comments.
