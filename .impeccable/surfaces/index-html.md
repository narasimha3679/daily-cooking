# Surface: Daily Cooking app shell, Recipes, Planner, Shopping list

Mode: Operate. Primary viewport: 390x844 phone, 360 to 430 first, then 768 and 1120 desktop. Audience: home cook on a busy weeknight, one handed, often offline.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A weekly kitchen ledger, not a recipe catalogue. The app opens on the cook's week and reads like a working kitchen book: a deep green masthead, a ruled week line, dish rows with tabular numbers, and a shopping list whose quantities sit in a clean column. It refuses the generic equal-card grid as the page's organising idea.

OWN-WORLD: Deep green ink #123528, warm cream paper #FBF5E8, terracotta action #B4492E, turmeric #C98A1E, curry leaf #4F6B3A. Georgia and the local serif stack for dish names and numerals, a system humanist sans for controls. One pixel warm rules, soft paper shadows, 12 to 16 px radii, tabular numerals, a full width terracotta rule under the masthead. No gradients, no glass, no icon tiles.

STORY: The cook sees the planned week, adds or swaps a dish in two taps, then shops from a list where the total and the buy amount agree. Loved dishes are visibly warm; Not for me dishes stay findable and reversible but never appear in planner picks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 390x844, a 64 px deep green masthead with the wordmark and a Week 1 button. Below it, a cream sticky toolbar with a 48 px search field, then category chips. The first recipe card starts by 220 px. A 64 px cream bottom bar holds Recipes, Planner and Shopping list with drawn SVG icons. At 900 px and above, the masthead and nav become a 232 px left rail and the recipe grid runs to three columns.

FORM: The assigned kitchen ledger, candidate 6 of 7 grounded directions. Durable world: South Indian kitchen weekly ledger, its ruled dates, paper stock and spice inks. seed:6d0da66c221c81cb

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
